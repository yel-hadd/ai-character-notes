Questions sellers ask

Can I pay from Morocco with my card?

Yes, with a Moroccan international card once the e-commerce allowance is on in your bank app. The personal allowance is about 20,000 MAD a year for all online purchases abroad. Agencies on Studio should use a company card.

Does it work for my COD product?

Yes. One product photo is enough for your presenter to hold the product and talk about it, and you also get statics in four sizes. For a usage demo, upload your own product clips and combine them with the presenter.

Can it speak Darija, Arabic or French?

Yes. Video models that generate the voice themselves, such as Veo 3.1 and Seedance, follow the language and accent you write in the prompt, for example "in Moroccan Darija with a Casablanca accent". You can also upload your own audio and the presenter lip-syncs to it, up to 10 minutes. Generate two versions and keep the most natural one.

Is it hard to use?

No. The app menus are in English, French and Arabic. Pick a template, add your product photo, and you get two versions to compare. No blank page.

Do I own the videos?

Yes. MakeInfluencer states that what you make is yours, with no watermarks, so you can run it in your paid ads.

Will AI ads beat my current creatives?

Your numbers will tell you. Run them against your current creatives with the same budget and audience, and keep the winners.
